About me

Quality, integrity and a job done right.

Above & Beyond Cleaning Co. is a woman-owned commercial janitorial company built on quality service, integrity and customer satisfaction.

What began in 2016 in Northern BC as The Clean Queen has grown into more than 10 years of experience in the cleaning industry. Today I serve Carstairs, Olds, Crossfield and Airdrie with cleaning tailored to what each client actually needs.
